# Nine Grahas in Vedic Astrology

> Sun through Saturn plus Rahu and Ketu

## Quick Answer

Classical Jyotish uses nine grahas: Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu, and Ketu. The outer planets are not part of the traditional nine-graha framework.

## Key Concepts

- Sun and Moon are luminaries, not “planets” in the modern astronomical sense.
- Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, and Saturn are the five visible classical planets.
- Rahu and Ketu are the lunar nodes, not physical bodies.
- Each graha has significations, rulerships, friendships, enmities, and exaltation points.
- Modern astrologers may study outer planets separately, but classical yogas use the nine-graha set.

## How to Use This Entry

Learn each graha’s core signification before combining it with sign, house, aspect, dignity, nakshatra, and dasha.

## FAQ

### Are Uranus, Neptune, and Pluto used in Jyotish?

Traditional Vedic astrology does not use them in its nine-graha core. Some contemporary practitioners study them as supplementary points.
