# What Is Vedic Astrology? Jyotish Basics

> Sidereal Jyotish: grahas, rashis, nakshatras, and dashas

## Quick Answer

Vedic astrology, commonly called Jyotish, is a classical Indian system that reads the sidereal zodiac, nine grahas, rashis, bhavas, nakshatras, planetary periods, and divisional charts as one structured whole.

## Key Concepts

- It uses the sidereal zodiac and an ayanamsa offset.
- The classical factors are the nine grahas, twelve rashis, twelve bhavas, and 27 nakshatras.
- Vimshottari and other dasha systems add timing layers.
- Divisional charts such as D9 refine specific topics.
- Traditional Jyotish reads tendencies and karma, not an unchangeable fate.

## How to Use This Entry

Start with the ascendant, Moon, and Sun; study placements, house lords, yogas, nakshatras, and the active dasha before interpreting timing.

## FAQ

### Is Vedic astrology scientifically proven?

No. Jyotish is best treated as a symbolic and cultural interpretive system, not as verified prediction or medical, legal, or financial advice.
