# Vedic Astrology Houses: The 12 Bhavas

> Lagna-based life areas and house lords in Jyotish

## Quick Answer

The twelve bhavas are Vedic astrological houses counted from the Lagna. They map self, wealth, courage, home, children, illness, partnership, longevity, dharma, career, gains, and loss or liberation.

## Key Concepts

- The 1st bhava describes body, vitality, and self.
- The 2nd, 5th, 9th, and 11th commonly relate to resources and gains.
- The 6th, 8th, and 12th are dusthanas and need careful judgment.
- The 7th covers partnership; the 10th covers karma and public action.
- House lords and their placements often carry more weight than house keywords alone.

## How to Use This Entry

Identify the bhava, its lord, occupants, aspects, strengths, and connections with dasha lords before interpreting a life area.

## FAQ

### Do Vedic houses always use whole signs?

Whole-sign Rasi references are common, but Jyotish also has bhava calculation methods; confirm the chart software’s system before comparison.
