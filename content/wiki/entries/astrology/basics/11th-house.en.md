# The 11th House in Astrology

## Core Meaning

The 11th house in astrology governs friends, communities, organizations, alliances, collective goals, long-term hopes, and future visions. It shows how a person exchanges support and influence in groups.

## Key Topics

- Friends and acquaintances
- Communities and organizations
- Shared goals and alliances
- Long-term hopes and visions
- Technology, resources, and collective influence

## Natural Sign and Ruler

The natural zodiac places Aquarius in the 11th house, ruled by Saturn classically and Uranus in modern practice.

## Planets in This House

Uranus likes innovative groups, Venus makes friendly bonds, Saturn chooses durable alliances, and Neptune needs discernment with idealization.

## How to Read It

Watch how the 11th house receives 10th-house achievement while keeping personal boundaries inside collective goals.
