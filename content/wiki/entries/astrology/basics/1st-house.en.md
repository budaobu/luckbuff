# The 1st House in Astrology

## Core Meaning

The 1st house in astrology governs identity, appearance, vitality, initiative, and first impressions. It begins at the ascendant and shows how a person enters a room and starts new things.

## Key Topics

- Identity and body awareness
- Vitality and initiative
- Beginnings and first reactions
- Appearance and personal style
- How a person enters a new room

## Natural Sign and Ruler

The natural zodiac places Aries and Mars in the 1st house; the chart-specific cusp sign still decides the actual ruler.

## Planets in This House

A planet here is visible. The Sun strengthens presence, the Moon makes feelings readable, Mars speeds action, and Saturn may begin as self-restraint.

## How to Read It

Start with the ascendant and planets here, trace the house ruler, and interpret identity themes against life stage.
