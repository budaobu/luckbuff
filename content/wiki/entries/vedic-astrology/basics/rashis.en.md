# Vedic Astrology Signs: The 12 Rashis

> Sidereal Aries through Pisces and their classical lords

## Quick Answer

The twelve rashis are the sidereal signs of Vedic astrology: Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius, and Pisces. Their degrees differ from tropical signs because of the ayanamsa.

## Key Concepts

- Aries–Virgo are ruled by Mars, Venus, Mercury, Moon, Sun, and Mercury.
- Libra–Pisces are ruled by Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Saturn, and Jupiter.
- Each rashi has element, mode, direction, body symbolism, and natural strengths.
- A planet’s result changes by rashi, nakshatra, house, aspects, and dignity.
- Sidereal dates are not identical to popular tropical Sun-sign dates.

## How to Use This Entry

Use an ayanamsa-based chart for Vedic rashis; then read the planet, rashi, house, lord, nakshatra, and current dasha together.

## FAQ

### Why are Vedic sign dates different?

Because Jyotish uses the sidereal zodiac, which accounts for precession, while popular Western Sun-sign dates use the tropical zodiac.
