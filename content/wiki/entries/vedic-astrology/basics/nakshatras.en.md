# Nakshatras in Vedic Astrology

> 27 lunar mansions, padas, and the Moon’s constellation

## Quick Answer

Nakshatras are 27 lunar mansions, each spanning 13 degrees 20 minutes of the sidereal zodiac. The Moon’s nakshatra is central to naming traditions, temperament, compatibility, and the starting point of Vimshottari dasha.

## Key Concepts

- Each nakshatra divides into four padas of 3 degrees 20 minutes.
- Every nakshatra has a symbolic deity, power, and planetary dasha lord.
- The Moon’s nakshatra is often more personally specific than the Sun sign.
- Nakshatras refine pronunciation-based name traditions and muhurta timing.
- A planet’s nakshatra changes its expression beyond the rashi alone.

## How to Use This Entry

Record each graha’s nakshatra and pada, then check the Moon nakshatra’s dasha lord and current planetary period.

## FAQ

### Why do nakshatra calculators sometimes differ?

Small differences in ayanamsa, birth-time rounding, or lunar-node conventions can move a sensitive point across a boundary.
