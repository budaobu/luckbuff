# Dashas in Vedic Astrology

> Planetary periods, Vimshottari sequence, and sub-periods

## Quick Answer

A dasha is a planetary period. Vimshottari dasha, the most widely used system, follows a 120-year sequence that begins from the Moon’s nakshatra lord, with the first period reduced by the Moon’s remaining degree in that nakshatra.

## Key Concepts

- Vimshottari order runs Ketu, Venus, Sun, Moon, Mars, Rahu, Jupiter, Saturn, and Mercury.
- The full Vimshottari cycle spans 120 years.
- Maha dashas are major periods; antardashas are sub-periods.
- The starting balance depends on the Moon’s exact nakshatra position.
- A dasha activates its lord’s chart role, not one fixed event.

## How to Use This Entry

Confirm the Moon’s nakshatra and balance first, then read the maha and antardasha lords by house, sign, dignity, aspects, and yogas.

## FAQ

### Does a Venus dasha always mean love?

No. Venus activates Venus’s significations and its chart position. It may describe relationships, aesthetics, finances, or other Venusian topics depending on the chart.
