# The 12 Houses in Astrology

## What the Houses Mean

Houses are the stages on which a birth chart happens. Signs describe style, planets describe energy, and houses show the life area where that energy operates. The ascendant begins the 1st house, and the remaining houses continue counterclockwise around the wheel.

A useful reading does not stop at one keyword. Look for four things: planets in the house, the sign on the house cusp, the house ruler’s condition, and any transits that activate that area.

## The 12 Houses at a Glance

| House | Focus | Common Topics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | Self and body | Appearance, initiative, identity, first impressions |
| 2nd | Money and values | Income, possessions, skills, self-worth, security |
| 3rd | Communication | Learning, writing, siblings, neighbors, short trips |
| 4th | Home and roots | Family, parents, property, private life, emotional base |
| 5th | Creativity and romance | Play, art, romance, children, healthy risk |
| 6th | Daily work and care | Routine, service, health, habits, craft |
| 7th | Partnership | Marriage, contracts, clients, one-to-one relationships |
| 8th | Sharing and transformation | Joint resources, intimacy, debt, crisis, deep change |
| 9th | Meaning and travel | Higher study, publishing, foreign places, philosophy |
| 10th | Career and public role | Vocation, achievement, authority, reputation |
| 11th | Community and future | Friends, networks, groups, hopes, long-term visions |
| 12th | The unconscious | Solitude, dreams, healing, hidden matters, retreat |

## Angular, Succedent, and Cadent

The 1st, 4th, 7th, and 10th houses are angular and tend to act visibly. The 2nd, 5th, 8th, and 11th are succedent and support continuity. The 3rd, 6th, 9th, and 12th are cadent and favor learning, adjustment, and reflection. None of these groups is inherently good or bad.

## Why House Systems Differ

Placidus, whole sign, Porphyry, and equal house systems divide the chart differently. A planet can therefore fall in a different house depending on the method. Placidus is common in modern Western astrology; whole sign is common in classical practice and simpler modern readings. Stay consistent when comparing charts.
