# The 3rd House in Astrology

## Core Meaning

The 3rd house in astrology governs thinking, speech, writing, siblings, neighbors, short trips, and everyday information. It shows how a mind organizes language and connects with its immediate world.

## Key Topics

- Speaking, writing, and study
- Siblings and neighbors
- Everyday conversation
- Short trips and commuting
- Basic skills and curiosity

## Natural Sign and Ruler

The natural zodiac places Gemini and Mercury in the 3rd house, a cadent area of observation, adjustment, and information flow.

## Planets in This House

Mercury sharpens language, the Moon connects learning with familiar people, Uranus brings new tools or leaps, and Neptune needs clearer wording.

## How to Read It

Notice how the cusp sign shapes tone, then see how planets here shape learning pace and information choices.
