# The 7th House in Astrology

## Core Meaning

The 7th house in astrology governs marriage, committed partners, clients, contracts, consultation, and visible one-to-one relationships. It also covers projection, balance, and negotiation.

## Key Topics

- Marriage and commitment
- Partners and key collaborators
- Contracts and clients
- Visible one-to-one relationships
- Negotiation, projection, and balance

## Natural Sign and Ruler

The natural zodiac places Libra and Venus in the 7th house at the descendant, the move from “I” to “we”.

## Planets in This House

Venus values relationship quality, Mars creates attraction and friction, Saturn leans toward commitment, and Uranus needs room to redefine terms.

## How to Read It

Do not reduce this house to marriage; it also describes clients, contracts, and open rivals.
