# The 8th House in Astrology

## Core Meaning

The 8th house in astrology governs shared money, intimacy, debt, inheritance, psychological transformation, crisis, and rebirth. It deals with deep exchange after personal boundaries are crossed.

## Key Topics

- Shared money and merged resources
- Debt, inheritance, and taxes
- Intimacy and trust
- Psychological depth and shadow
- Transformation after crisis

## Natural Sign and Ruler

The natural zodiac places Scorpio in the 8th house, ruled by Mars classically and Pluto in modern practice.

## Planets in This House

Pluto intensifies control-and-rebirth themes, Venus values intimacy, Jupiter can enlarge resource flows, and Saturn demands boundaries.

## How to Read It

Judge the intensity through aspects. The 8th house describes transformation, not guaranteed disaster.
