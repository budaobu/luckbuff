# The 4th House in Astrology

## Core Meaning

The 4th house in astrology governs home, family, property, parents, ancestry, private life, and emotional security. Anchored by the IC, it describes the ground a person returns to.

## Key Topics

- Home and private space
- Parents and family influence
- Property and living situation
- Emotional security
- Early memory and belonging

## Natural Sign and Ruler

The natural zodiac places Cancer and the Moon in the 4th house; the IC sits here as the most private point of the chart.

## Planets in This House

The Moon deepens attachment needs, Saturn can add duty or early restraint, Uranus needs flexibility, and Neptune may blur boundaries.

## How to Read It

Read the IC sign and planets here, then follow the house ruler to see how family themes connect outward.
