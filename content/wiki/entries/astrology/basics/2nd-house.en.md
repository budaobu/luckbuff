# The 2nd House in Astrology

## Core Meaning

The 2nd house in astrology governs income, possessions, earning skills, values, and security. It shows how resources are built and how money is tied to self-worth.

## Key Topics

- Income and available resources
- Possessions and savings
- Marketable skills
- Personal values
- Security beneath money habits

## Natural Sign and Ruler

The natural zodiac places Taurus and Venus in the 2nd house: a pattern of maintenance, accumulation, and bodily satisfaction.

## Planets in This House

Venus favors comfort and aesthetics, Jupiter can expand income or spending, Saturn adds discipline, and Pluto deals with deep control or restructuring.

## How to Read It

Read the sign, ruler, aspects, and real habits together; the 2nd house is not automatically fixed salary.
