# The 10th House in Astrology

## Core Meaning

The 10th house in astrology governs vocation, public role, achievement, authority, responsibility, and long-term reputation. Anchored by the MC, it shows the contribution a person allows to be seen.

## Key Topics

- Career and vocation
- Public role and achievement
- Reputation and visibility
- Authority, bosses, and institutions
- Long-term responsibility

## Natural Sign and Ruler

The natural zodiac places Capricorn and Saturn in the 10th house; the MC here is the chart’s public peak.

## Planets in This House

The Sun seeks accomplishment, Saturn accumulates slowly, Neptune can idealize a role, and Pluto often reworks power structures.

## How to Read It

Combine the MC sign, planets, ruler, and 6th-house routine to see whether vocation is one path or several tracks.
