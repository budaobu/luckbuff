# Vedic Astrology Chart: How to Read the D1 Chart

> Lagna, rashi chart, bhavas, Moon chart, and divisional charts

## Quick Answer

The Vedic birth chart, or D1 Rasi chart, starts at the ascendant (Lagna) and maps the nine grahas through the sidereal signs. It is the base for house analysis, Moon-chart reading, divisional charts, and dasha timing.

## Key Concepts

- D1 establishes the Lagna and the twelve sign-based references.
- Rashi means sign; bhava refers to a life area and may be calculated separately.
- The Moon chart, or Chandra Lagna, is often read beside the ascendant chart.
- D9 Navamsa refines marriage, inner maturity, and planetary strength.
- Vimshottari dasha uses the Moon’s nakshatra to establish planetary periods.

## How to Use This Entry

Read Lagna, Moon, and Sun first; then study sign, house, lord, aspects, yogas, nakshatra, and dasha before making any timing statement.

## FAQ

### Can a Vedic chart be read with a Western chart?

Yes, but do not mix coordinates in one calculation. Keep tropical and sidereal models separate and compare their conclusions only after each is read correctly.
