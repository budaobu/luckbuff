# Navamsa Chart in Vedic Astrology

> D9, marriage symbolism, planetary strength, and vargottama

## Quick Answer

The Navamsa, or D9, divides each sidereal sign into nine parts. It is a key divisional chart for marriage, inner maturity, hidden strength, and verifying whether a planet’s promise in D1 is supported.

## Key Concepts

- Each navamsa spans 3 degrees 20 minutes within its sign.
- D9 is commonly read with D1, not as a replacement for it.
- A planet strong in Navamsa is more able to deliver its D1 promise.
- A planet in the same sign in D1 and D9 is called vargottama.
- D9 often refines partnership and inner development, but other divisional charts cover other topics.

## How to Use This Entry

Compare a planet’s D1 and D9 sign, dignity, house symbolism, aspects, and dasha activation before judging its strength.

## FAQ

### Is Navamsa only a marriage chart?

No. Marriage is a common D9 topic, but the chart also helps assess planetary strength and inner maturation across the life.
