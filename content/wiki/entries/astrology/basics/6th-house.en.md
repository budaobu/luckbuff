# The 6th House in Astrology

## Core Meaning

The 6th house in astrology governs daily work, routines, health maintenance, service, craft, and the systems that organize responsibility. It links bodily rhythm to sustainable effectiveness.

## Key Topics

- Daily work and duties
- Habits and systems
- Health maintenance
- Service and craft
- Coworkers and routine collaboration

## Natural Sign and Ruler

The natural zodiac places Virgo and Mercury in the 6th house: analysis, repair, and sustainable rhythm.

## Planets in This House

Mercury optimizes, Mars needs physical outlet, Saturn builds discipline, and Chiron often marks a sensitive point needing long-term care.

## How to Read It

The 6th house is not a medical diagnosis. Use it to understand habits and stress patterns; health decisions need professionals.
