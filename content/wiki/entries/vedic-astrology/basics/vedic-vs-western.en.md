# Western Astrology vs Vedic Astrology

> Tropical and sidereal charts use different reference points

## Quick Answer

Western astrology commonly uses the tropical zodiac tied to seasons, while Vedic astrology uses the sidereal zodiac referenced to fixed stars. The resulting planetary signs often differ by roughly 24 degrees today.

## Key Concepts

- Western tropical astrology begins Aries at the March equinox.
- Vedic sidereal astrology corrects for the precession of the equinoxes.
- Modern Western work often emphasizes psychology, aspects, and house systems.
- Jyotish emphasizes classical strengths, yogas, nakshatras, and dashas.
- Neither system is scientifically proven more accurate than the other.

## How to Use This Entry

Compare both only after confirming the zodiac, ayanamsa, house method, and birth-time accuracy; avoid mixing tropical signs with sidereal placements in one judgment.

## FAQ

### Which one should a beginner learn first?

Learn one system first and learn its own rules. If you later study the other, treat the changed zodiac and technique base as a separate model.
